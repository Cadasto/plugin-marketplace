# Contact Cadasto

The Cadasto Plugin Marketplace is a [Cadasto](https://www.cadasto.com/) product. Cadasto's [About](https://www.cadasto.com/about-us/) page introduces the company.

## Company email

Write to [info@cadasto.com](mailto:info@cadasto.com).

## Sales enquiries

Cadasto sells only through authorised resellers, so there is no direct sale. Use the [form on cadasto.com](https://www.cadasto.com/contact/) to reach Cadasto or the reseller for your region. This site carries no form of its own.

## Product issues

A catalog pin or a page on this site belongs in [Cadasto/plugin-marketplace](https://github.com/Cadasto/plugin-marketplace). A bug in a plugin belongs in that plugin's repository. [Build the marketplace site](contributing.md) names both.
